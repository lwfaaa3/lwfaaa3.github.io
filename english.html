Deshan Li, Ph.D.

I am an Associate Professor and Master's Supervisor at the School of Business, Sichuan Normal University. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Sichuan University in 2018. My research lies in macroeconomic theory and policy, population economics, and resource and environmental economics, with a focus on how demographic transition and technological innovation shape long-term economic growth.

I have served as Principal Investigator on projects funded by the National Social Science Fund of China, the Ministry of Education, and provincial and municipal programmes. My work has appeared in CSSCI and SCI (JCR Q1) journals — 30+ peer-reviewed papers, including 10+ as first or corresponding author — and I have published two books. I was named a CNKI Top 1% Highly Cited Scholar in 2024 and 2025.
